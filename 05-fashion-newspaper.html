WHAT ARE YOU WEARING TONIGHT?

A definitive field guide to becoming the main character for one evening without spending ₹35,000 on a silhouette you will photograph once and never re-wear.

The economics of dressing for collegiate ceremony have long bordered on the absurd. Four years of university life inevitably culminate in a succession of monumental evenings: the annual festival star night, the department farewell ball, the society debut, the midnight after-party. Each occasion demands an entrance that commands attention.

Yet the traditional response—purchasing an expensive garment destined for permanent retirement in a cramped hostel cupboard—is an obsolete ritual.

"To possess clothing that exists solely for a single photograph is an obsolete burden. The modern intellect rents the moment."

WEDELIA proposes a civilized alternative: a revolving couture archive dispatched directly to campus residential gates. Outfits arrive twenty-four hours before call time, accompanied by a complimentary secondary backup size to banish sizing anxiety forever. You wear it into the night, get photographed under the strobes, and return it unwashed.
