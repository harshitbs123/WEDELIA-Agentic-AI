WEDELIA

ART DIRECTION EXPLORATIONS // 10 RADICAL DIRECTIONS
PROJECT: WEDELIA (AI)
REFERENCES: UNITED ATHLE / THE FASHION POST / DROPTOKYO
STATUS: READY FOR CREATIVE SELECTION

Ten completely different, purely static editorial directions for WEDELIA. Each direction explores a distinct typographic personality, spatial tension, navigation structure, and Japanese fashion-magazine art direction. Review each prototype, test on mobile/desktop, and pick the visual direction for WEDELIA. 日本のファッション雑誌と実験的デジタルスタジオの交差点。10の視覚的プロトタイプ。

PROTOTYPE 01 東京エディトリアル

Tokyo Editorial

Off-white background, black serif typography, huge titles, tiny Japanese metadata, asymmetrical off-grid photography, and Popeye/Droptokyo-style vertical breathing room.

Warm Off-White Mincho Serif Asymmetric Whitespace
PROTOTYPE 02 技術目録

Brutalist Fashion Index

Experimental archive catalog. Pure white background, razor-sharp 1px black borders, monospaced metadata tables, oversized numbers breaking grid cells, and strict utilitarian elegance.

1px Hairline Grid Monospace Data Archive Catalog
PROTOTYPE 03 紅と黒

Red / Black Tokyo

High-contrast Tokyo after-dark editorial. Pure white and dense obsidian black punctuated by a single intense vermilion red used strictly as an editorial marking pen.

Vermilion Red Ikko Tanaka Tribute Heavy Display Sans
PROTOTYPE 04 縦型ポスター

Vertical Japanese Poster

Built like a series of exhibition posters. Vertical typography (writing-mode: vertical-rl), narrow image banners, oversized kanji graphic anchors, and Ginza gallery paper composition.

Vertical Writing-Mode Parchment Cream Poster Layout
PROTOTYPE 05 新聞社説

Fashion Newspaper

High-end fashion broadsheet chronicle. Multi-column journalistic layout, editorial drop caps, classified rental index, double-ruled frames, and sophisticated cultural essaying.

Broadsheet Columns Editorial Headlines Classified Rental Index
PROTOTYPE 06 空白の美

White Void

Extreme luxury minimalism. 85%+ screen void, tiny corner metadata, one colossal monolithic photograph, and the words "WEAR THE MOMENT". Expensive attitude without visual noise.

Extreme Whitespace Kenya Hara Influence Monolithic Scale
PROTOTYPE 07 コンタクトシート

Tokyo Street Contact Sheet

Fashion photographer's working darkroom proof sheet. 35mm film rebate frames, exposure stamps, Shibuya coordinates, hand-marked grease pencil crop frames, and raw documentation.

Darkroom Emulsion 35mm Film Frames Grease Pencil Marks
PROTOTYPE 08 電脳端末

Fashion Terminal

Japanese fashion magazine meets retro-futuristic mainframe terminal. Phosphor bone text on dark obsidian, text indexes, bracket navigation, and ASCII coordinate windows. Not cyberpunk.

Monospace Terminal Bracket Interface Coordinate Systems
PROTOTYPE 09 切り絵コラージュ

Collage / Cut Paper

Tactile art director's pasteboard. Overlapping image crops, typography crossing photography boundaries, intentional misalignment, raw paper tones, and avant-garde zine pacing.

Paper Cut Geometry Overlapping Planes Zine Aesthetic
PROTOTYPE 10 異形の空間

The Strange One

Defiantly unconventional. Rotated edge-running vertical navigation, oversized typography partially bleeding outside the viewport, spatial installation pillars, and unforgettable art-gallery tension.

Viewport Bleed Edge Navigation Non-Euclidean Spatial Grid
